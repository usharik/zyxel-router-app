Unofficial app to manage your Zyxel home router from your phone, directly over your home network.

• Overview: internet status, external IP, line speed, uptime.
• Devices: everyone on your network with type, Wi-Fi signal, traffic, IP and MAC. Devices that went offline stay in the list. Unknown devices are identified by manufacturer and by what they report about themselves on the network: name, model, services.
• Wi-Fi networks: band, channel, security, guest networks.
• Share any network, including the guest one, with a QR code: the password is read from the router only when you ask for it and goes only to the app you choose.
• Parental control: schedules by day and time for chosen devices, turned on and off with one tap.
• Routes and the router's system log with statistics by day, week and month.
• Router reboot.

No cloud and no account: device lists, network settings, passwords and the log from your router never leave your phone unless you share them yourself. The router password is stored encrypted on your phone only.

No router at hand? Tap “Try without a router” on the login screen to explore the app with built-in sample data.

Tested on VMG8623-T50B. It uses the same web interface as many other Zyxel routers, so it should also work with models such as VMG8825-T50, VMG3625-T50B, VMG4005-B50A, AX7501-B0, EX5601-T1, NR5103E, NR5307, NR7101, NR7102, NR7302, FWA505, FWA510, FWA710, LTE3202-M437, LTE5398-M904, LTE7490-M904. Available features depend on the model and firmware.

The app is free with ads; a one-time purchase removes them.
